How to Spot a Phishing Email

  1. Check the sender address and domain spelling.
  2. Be cautious with unexpected urgency or threats.
  3. Inspect links without opening them.
  4. Be careful with credential or personal-information requests.
  5. Do not open unexpected attachments.
  6. Watch for generic greetings and unusual formatting.
  7. Verify unusual payment requests through a trusted channel.
  8. Consider the full context rather than one indicator.
Before You Click:

Pause → inspect sender → inspect destination → verify through a trusted channel → report if appropriate.
